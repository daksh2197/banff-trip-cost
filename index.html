            Read this before you book

            This is a calculator, not financial advice. It adds up what you type and the figures above. It does not check availability, and it does not know what a rental company will charge at the counter.

            Fuel ratings are test-cycle numbers from Natural Resources Canada. Real use varies with speed, weather, air conditioning and the vehicle's age, and cold weather can raise it a lot. The calculator adds no winter uplift of its own.

            Not affiliated with Parks Canada, Natural Resources Canada, the Government of Alberta or any rental company. No rental company is named, ranked or linked here.

            Everything runs on your device. What you type is not sent anywhere. A shared link carries your numbers after the "#", which browsers never send to a website.
