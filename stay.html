A hotel or an Airbnb or VRBO? The nightly rate is the headline. This adds the fees, the taxes, parking, food and the drive in.

    Fuel is only needed if a stay adds driving. There is no default fuel price: type what you expect to pay.

    2. The stays

    Get prices for the same dates from: Expedia, Booking.com, Airbnb and VRBO. Compare Banff with Canmore: a cheaper bed further out can cost more once you add the drive.

    Read this before you book

    This is a calculator, not advice. It adds what you type. It does not check availability, work out taxes, or know a property's rules.

    Extra driving uses the formula from the trip page: km times L/100 km divided by 100, times the fuel price. Food is your own guess for each place, because a kitchen changes what a day of eating costs.

    Not affiliated with any hotel, booking site, Airbnb or VRBO. No property is named or ranked except the ones you type.

    Everything runs on your device. A shared link carries your numbers after the "#", which browsers never send to a website.
